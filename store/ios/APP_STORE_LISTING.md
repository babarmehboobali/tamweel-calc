Loan Calculator: Tamweel & EMI helps you plan personal, auto and mortgage financing in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, the GCC and worldwide — with every calculation kept on your iPhone.

1. Choose your country & mode (Saudi, UAE, GCC or Worldwide)
2. Enter salary & obligations (basic, allowances, loans, cards)
3. See your maximum financing & DBR on a live gauge

• Full Arabic (RTL) and English, light & dark themes
